# 📖 Smart Internal Knowledge Assistant — User Guide

Welcome to your **Smart Internal Knowledge Assistant**! This guide is written for everyone in the company. You do not need any technical skills or computer background to use this app.

---

## 🌟 What is This App and Why Does It Exist?

Have you ever spent hours searching through shared Google Drives, endless Slack channels, or Notion pages just to find the company’s annual leave policy, VPN setup steps, or holiday calendar? 

The **Smart Internal Knowledge Assistant** is your friendly digital workplace companion. It acts like a 24/7 knowledgeable colleague who has read every official company handbook, policy document, and guide. Whenever you have a question, it finds the exact answer for you in seconds.

---

## 🚀 Key Advantages — How Does It Make Your Life Easier?

* ⚡ **Instant Answers, Zero Waiting:** No need to send an email to HR or IT and wait days for a reply. Get immediate answers anytime, day or night.
* 🛡️ **100% Trustworthy & Verified:** The assistant never guesses or makes things up. Every answer includes verifiable citation badges (like `[1]`, `[2]`). Clicking on a badge instantly shows you the official company document it came from.
* 🌐 **Bilingual (English & Myanmar):** Ask in English or Myanmar (Burmese). The assistant understands both languages naturally and replies in your preferred language.
* 🎫 **One-Click Support Tickets:** If a question cannot be answered from existing company documents, you can open an internal support ticket with the right team directly from your chat screen with one single click.

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step How-To (5 Simple Steps)

Using the assistant takes less than 30 seconds:

```
Step 1: Open Chat  ➡️  Step 2: Pick Language  ➡️  Step 3: Ask Question  ➡️  Step 4: Check Proof  ➡️  Step 5: Feedback / Ticket
```

1. **Step 1: Open the Chat Screen**  
   Click on **AI Assistant** in the left menu. You will see a welcoming message and a clean chat screen.

2. **Step 2: Choose Your Language**  
   Look at the top-right of the chat screen. You can switch between **EN** (English) and **မြန်မာ** (Myanmar) at any time.

3. **Step 3: Ask Your Question**  
   Type your question in the text box at the bottom and press `Enter` (or click the Send airplane icon). You can also click any of the handy suggestion pills at the top (like *"What is our annual leave policy?"* or *"ကုမ္ပဏီ VPN ဘယ်လိုချိတ်ဆက်ရမလဲ?"*).

4. **Step 4: Read the Answer & Check Official Documents**  
   As the assistant answers, you will see blue citation badges like `[1]`. Click on any badge to slide open the **Reference Document** panel and see the exact paragraphs from the company handbook.

5. **Step 5: Give Feedback or Request Extra Help**  
   - Click the Thumbs-Up 👍 or Thumbs-Down 👎 to help the system improve.
   - If you need a human to help you further, click **Open Ticket** to submit an inquiry straight to the IT or HR department.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can everyone else in the company see my chat questions?
**No.** Your conversations are private to your user account within your company organization.

### What if the assistant says it cannot find the information?
If the information is not in our company's uploaded documents, the assistant will tell you honestly and provide contact details for the relevant team (e.g., HR or IT Helpdesk), along with a one-click button to open a support ticket.

### Do I need to type in formal language?
**No.** Just type naturally, like you are messaging a coworker on Slack or Teams.
